# Disclaimer

## Who develops this app

This is an independently developed, unofficial plugin for Projectivy Launcher. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Projectivy Launcher developers, Google, or YouTube. Product names identify the services the app works with.

## Content and permissions

Use only videos you own or have permission to download and display. Copyright and other rights may apply even when a video is publicly accessible. The app asks you to acknowledge this before your first YouTube download. Acknowledgment records your choice on the device and grants no content rights.

Downloads are explicitly user-initiated. The app writes clips to local device storage and grants the installed Projectivy Launcher read access to the selected clip. The app has no developer upload destination or cloud processing service. Repositories and APKs contain no video files.
